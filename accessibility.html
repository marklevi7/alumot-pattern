Accessibility Statement

Effective: 1 January 2026

We're committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities.

Standards

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level.

What we do

  • Provide alternative text for images.
  • Support keyboard navigation.
  • Use a responsive layout that adapts to mobile and desktop.
  • Maintain clear typographic hierarchy and sufficient contrast.

Help and feedback

If something on the site isn't working for you, email [email protected] and we'll do our best to address it quickly.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility isn't a one-time fix — we treat it as an ongoing part of the work and welcome your input.
